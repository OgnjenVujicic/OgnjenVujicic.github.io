Privacy policy

Effective 24 September 2026

rclone-notes is a personal tool used only by its owner to sync their own notes with their own Google Drive.

Data accessed

rclone-notes accesses only the files in the Google Drive folder its owner has designated for notes.

How data is used

Files are read and written solely to keep that folder identical between the owner's devices and the owner's Google Drive. No other use is made of them.

Storage and sharing

Notes are stored only on the owner's devices and in the owner's Google Drive. Google authorization tokens are stored locally on the owner's computers. No data is sent to any server operated by rclone-notes, and no data is sold, shared with or transferred to any third party. No analytics or tracking are used.

Google API Services

rclone-notes' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
